# The meshes at the bend set (issue #265, Stage C)

Generated by `tools/bend_survey.ts --page`; every figure is the tool's. The bend set: every link of every chain a part names turned by +θ about its joint (each on top of its parent's), and the same at −θ. θ is 15° per link (the fallback: a physics constraint's reachable amplitude is not derivable from the constraint itself), and on the trailer also the film's measured bends (skirt 24°, legs 12°, long hair 10°, fringe 9°, the waving sleeve 32°; any other chain 15°). The reference is denser than the lattice: the part's art at every stride-th pixel centre (at most 20000 per part), each skinned by the part's own weight rule at that point. Per mesh and part: the largest displacement from the reference over both poses (rig px), the triangles whose signed area flips or vanishes, and the stretch (the largest r or 1/r over every triangle edge, r = posed over setup length). Stage C idle ∪ bend: Stage C's insertion with the bend held to the lattice's own reading per part (displacement no larger, no more flipped triangles than the lattice) once the idle's 1.0 px holds, each bend site at least 4 px from every vertex, and an insertion that worsens the idle above its bound, adds a flipped triangle or (a bend site) enlarges the bend reading undone and its site skipped; cap 64 insertions per part (and 4 × that many attempts).
