# The automatic mesh policy on the public parts: outline, art threshold and reduction bounds (issue #266, Stage A)

Generated by `bun tools/auto_policy_survey.ts`; every figure below is the tool's, none typed. The installed rig-c 2.40.0. The parts are `demo` and `scarf` (parts-examples at commit 842c5b373fb155e588c51e458efa6eb5d5db6c73, `bun run fetch-examples`, each with its tracked `examples/<key>/config.json`) and the trailer (https://github.com/rig-c/trailer at commit a99d976ce454577d6145e8e174ce850c581491a7, its `inputs/` and `inputs/requirements.json`). Every mesh part of each config, switched alone to `auto` (`{ auto, r, segments }`, as the production trial switches; a tracked region is not carried) under each arm, through `assembleStage` and `rigStage`; then each example with every part that built alone under the arm switched at once, through `checkStage` too. Cells ran on: Nova, a remote Linux machine (WSL), in the shared pool, not exclusive: one job per example and arm, 31 runs (demo base twice: its first run counted a replayed part's last replay as the final pass, which this tool no longer does; the five trailer jobs that also ran before that fix wrote no part at a replayed step, so their counts read the same).

The contact sheets are read before the tables: [demo](auto-policy-survey-demo.png), [scarf](auto-policy-survey-scarf.png), [trailer](auto-policy-survey-trailer.png) — each part alone under each arm, the mesh over the part's art, the outline in red, the art at alpha 1 to 8 in orange.

## The arms

Each arm is one change over `trialPolicy` (tools/production_trial.ts: source tolerance = margin = 2, maxBoundaryDeviation 2, every overshoot bound 5, coverage 1, undercut 0 on the result, motion and residual 1 rig px, the Stage B opt-ins). `trace4`/`trace8` are not the card's: they read its "dev" row the other way (the source traced at N) and stand beside it. A part whose art is several 4-connected islands is given `source.stray` = its second largest island, on every arm alike (the production trial's manual retry, here by rule).
